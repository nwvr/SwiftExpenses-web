Add a receipt

Each expense is a PDF receipt plus details such as vendor, date and cost. The PDF is stored inside the library, so you can delete or move the original file afterward.

  1. Click the Add button + at the top of the sidebar, or choose File > New Expense (or press ⌘N).
  2. Drag the receipt PDF onto the right side of the window, or click Choose PDF. SwiftExpenses reads the receipt and fills in what it finds.
  3. Enter the expense report name. Click the arrow next to the field to choose an existing report, or type a new name to start a new report.
  4. Choose a trip type, and check where the expense happened (city, state, country).
  5. Check the expense type, vendor, dates, cost and currency, and correct anything that’s wrong.
  6. Click Save Expense.

The Save Expense button becomes available after you add a PDF and a report name.

On iPhone and iPad

Tap +, then tap Scan Receipt to photograph a paper receipt with the camera (it’s saved as a PDF), or Choose PDF to pick one from Files, Mail attachments saved to Files, or iCloud Drive.

How receipts are read

If Apple Intelligence is turned on, SwiftExpenses uses it to read the vendor, dates, total, currency, place and expense type. It runs on your Mac or iPhone; the receipt isn’t sent anywhere. Otherwise it uses vendor templates and simple text rules, which find less.

Scanned receipts are read with on-device text recognition first. A note under the receipt says what was read and how. Click Read Again to read the receipt again, replacing the details.

Tip: For flights, check that the dates are the travel dates, not the booking date. For rides, check whether the amount should include the tip.

If the receipt was saved before

When you add a PDF that’s already in the library, SwiftExpenses tells you which report it’s in. Click Cancel to drop it, or Use It Anyway to save it again.

If the PDF is a scan

You’re told when a PDF has no readable text, for example a scanned paper receipt. You can still save it.

Note: Your original PDF file is only read, never changed.
