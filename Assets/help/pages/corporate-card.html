Expenses paid with the corporate credit card

If you have a corporate credit card, the company pays its bill. Those expenses still belong in your expense report, so the company can reconcile them with the card account, but you don’t get the money back.

Mark an expense

  1. Open the expense (or the receipt in Unassigned Receipts, or a new expense).
  2. Under Payment, turn on Paid with corporate credit card.
  3. Enter the last 4 digits of the card. SwiftExpenses remembers them, so the next card expense gets them filled in.
  4. Save the expense.

In the report

  • The receipts table and the Excel workbooks get a Corporate CC column with the amount paid with the card. Reports without card expenses look as before.
  • Under the totals: Paid with Corporate Credit Card (with the card’s last 4 digits) and Amount Due to Traveler, which is the combined total minus the card expenses.
  • In the PDF report, the receipts you paid yourself come first. The card receipts follow at the end, after a page titled Corporate Credit Card Receipts that lists them for the company’s reconciliation.
  • The bottom of the report window shows Corporate CC and Due to you; the summary workbook adds both columns for every report.
