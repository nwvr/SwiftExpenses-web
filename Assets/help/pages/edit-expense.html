View, change or delete an expense

Select a report in the sidebar to see its receipts. Select a receipt to show its details and PDF next to the list.

Change an expense

  1. Select the report in the sidebar, then select the receipt.
  2. Change any of the details. To move the expense to another report, change its expense report name.
  3. Click Save (or press ⌘S).

City and route

When a receipt is read, its city (for hotels) and, for flights, trains and rides, where the trip starts and ends are filled in. The route appears in the From → To column and in the From and To fields of an expense. To fill in receipts you added before, choose Tools > Fill In Cities and Routes from Receipts (on the web: the Fill In Cities and Routes button of a report). Only empty details are filled in.

View the receipt

The receipt PDF appears below the details. Click Enlarge on the preview to see it large: on a Mac it opens in its own window (which you can make full screen or move to another display), on iPhone and iPad it fills the screen (tap Done to go back), and in the web app it fills the page (click Close or press Esc). The same works for receipts in Unassigned Receipts and in New Expense.

To open it in Preview, click the Open Receipt button in the toolbar, or Control-click the receipt in the list and choose View Receipt.

Delete an expense

Select the receipt, click the Delete button (the trash can) in the toolbar, then click Delete. The expense and its stored PDF are removed from the library.
