Export and email a report

Exporting creates two files in the library’s reports folder: a PDF to submit and an Excel workbook with the same numbers.

  1. Select the report in the sidebar.
  2. Click Export in the toolbar.
    • Mac: choose Export PDF and Excel (the files are shown in Finder), Export and Open PDF, or Export and Email. Show Reports Folder shows all earlier exports.
    • iPhone and iPad: the files are made and the share sheet opens, so you can save them to Files, AirDrop or mail them.

What’s in the PDF

  • The expense summary, the MS&IE days (unless the report has no MS&IE) and the totals, with the logo at the top of every page.
  • A Data Sources page naming the exchange-rate and per-diem sources and when they were last updated.
  • Every receipt PDF, in date order.

The Excel workbook has the same summary with live formulas, every receipt’s raw data, and the data sources. Files are named after the report and the time of export, so earlier exports are kept.

Email a report

Choose Email Report. If Microsoft Outlook is installed, a new Outlook message opens with the PDF and Excel file attached; otherwise your default mail app is used. The first time, macOS asks whether SwiftExpenses may control Outlook. On iPhone and iPad, use the Share button instead.

Note: Every time you save a change, SwiftExpenses also updates <report name>_Expense_Report.xlsx in the report’s folder, as the earlier Python app did.
