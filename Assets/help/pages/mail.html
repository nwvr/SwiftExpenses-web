Find receipts in Mail (Mac)

On a Mac, SwiftExpenses can look through a mailbox in Apple Mail or classic Microsoft Outlook for ride, flight and hotel receipts, and add the ones you choose to Unassigned Receipts. Your mail is read on your Mac only; nothing is changed or sent anywhere.

Turn it on

  1. Choose SwiftExpenses > Settings, then click Mail.
  2. Select Find receipts in Mail, and choose your mail app and the period to search by default.
  3. For Apple Mail, give SwiftExpenses Full Disk Access in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access, then quit and reopen SwiftExpenses. SwiftExpenses reads Mail’s saved messages directly, so even a large mailbox is searched in seconds and Mail doesn’t need to be open.

Find receipts

  1. Select Unassigned Receipts in the sidebar, then click Find in Mail.
  2. Choose the mailbox and a period: Year to date, the last 30 or 90 days, or a Date range (changing the From or To date switches to a date range). Then click Search. For Outlook, allow SwiftExpenses to control Outlook the first time.
  3. Check the receipts you want, then click Add to Unassigned Receipts. A mail’s PDF attachment is used when it has one; otherwise the mail itself is saved as the receipt PDF.

Choose vendors, or add your own

Under Search for, select which vendors to look for: Uber and Lyft, airlines and trains, hotels, car rentals, and your own vendors. To add a vendor, click Add Vendor and enter:

  • Sender: the address the receipts come from (for example billing@acme.com), or just the domain (acme.com).
  • Only mails with an attachment: only mails with an attachment count.
  • Subject contains: words such as “invoice, receipt”; a mail counts if its subject has any of them.

Your vendors are saved, so you can search for just one vendor again later. You can also edit them in Settings > Mail.

Updated receipts

Uber and Lyft often send a ride receipt again after you add a tip, and airlines and hotels send a new mail when a booking changes. Only the latest mail for each ride or booking is selected; earlier versions are marked Replaced. Mails you added before are marked Already added.

Note: Outlook can be read only when “New Outlook” is turned off. If you see a message that SwiftExpenses isn’t allowed, check Full Disk Access (Apple Mail) or Automation (Outlook) in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
