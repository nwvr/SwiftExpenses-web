Add meals and incidentals (MS&IE)

MS&IE is the daily allowance for meals and incidental expenses. SwiftExpenses works it out for each day of a trip from the official per-diem rates.

  1. Select the report in the sidebar, then click MS&IE at the top.
  2. Check the trip start and end dates. They come from the travel itself: the first and last day of the flights, trains, hotel stays and rental cars. Rides, meals and other one-day receipts don’t change them, so an Uber home after midnight doesn’t add a day. When the saved days differ from the travel dates, Use Travel Dates sets them; the first and last day become travel days.
  3. For each day, check the city and country, and select the meals that were provided to you (B for breakfast, L for lunch, D for dinner). Each day’s location comes from the hotel stay of that night; the last day uses the last hotel. Days marked No hotel (for example a stay with friends) take the location from a receipt of that day or the nearest hotel stay, so check them. Fill Locations from Hotels sets all days again from the hotel stays.
  4. Click Save MS&IE.

How the amount is calculated

  • Rate: GSA rates for places in the United States. GSA lists areas by city and county: a town it doesn’t name gets the rate of its county’s area (the county is looked up once with OpenStreetMap), and a town in no listed area gets the standard rate; State Department rates for other countries.
  • Travel days: the first and last day of a trip are selected as travel days and get 75% of the rate (41 CFR 301-11.20). You can change which days are travel days.
  • Provided meals: a provided meal is deducted using the official breakdown: GSA’s M&IE breakdown in the US (for example $80 = breakfast $20, lunch $22, dinner $33, incidentals $5) and GSA’s foreign breakdown table abroad (above $265: breakfast 15%, lunch 25%, dinner 40%). A day never drops below the incidental expenses amount. Meals served on a plane or train and free hotel breakfasts are not deducted (41 CFR 301-11.21).
  • None: select None for a day with no allowance at all.

After changing a city, state or country, press Return to look up the rate again. Click Recalculate to redo every day.

Reports without MS&IE

Turn on This report does not include MS&IE at the top of the report. The report’s MS&IE total is then $0, in both the report and the Summary.
