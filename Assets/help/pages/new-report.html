Make an expense report from your receipts

New Expense Report takes you through three steps, from new receipts to a report that’s ready to check and submit.

  1. Click New Expense Report at the bottom of the sidebar (Mac: also File > New Expense Report, ⇧⌘N; web: at the top of the sidebar).
  2. Add new receipts for the trip, as in Upload Receipts, or click Continue if they are already in Unassigned Receipts.
  3. Choose the receipts. Unassigned receipts are grouped by date: a new group starts after a gap of more than three days, and a hotel stay keeps its nights together. The first group is selected; use Select Group or tick single receipts. The total of the selection is shown.
  4. Name the report. A name is suggested from the hotel’s city (or where the travel went) and the month, for example “Kuala Lumpur October 2026”. Change it as you like, or add the receipts to an open report whose dates match.
  5. Click Create Report. The report opens: check the MS&IE days, then export it or submit it for review.
