Submit reports for review

You can keep all your trips in one library. The sidebar groups reports by the year and month their trip started, and reports you have handed in are marked as submitted.

Submit for Review

  1. Click Submit for Review — in Summary, in a report, or choose File > Submit for Review (⇧⌘S).
  2. Select the reports: all reports not submitted yet are listed and selected; use All and None, or tick single reports. Select Also show reports already submitted to send one again.
  3. Enter the submission name (for example “Submission October 2026”) and date, and click Create Zip. When the zip is saved, the reports are marked as submitted under that name.

What the zip contains

  • One report: its Excel workbook and its PDF report with all receipts, at the top of the zip.
  • Several reports: Expense_Reports_Summary.xlsx (every report’s totals, trip dates and submission, plus a sheet per report) and a folder per report with its Excel workbook and PDF report.
  • Always: a Review Library folder, a copy of the library holding just these reports with all receipts, MS&IE days and rates, and a README.txt. The reviewer opens the Review Library folder in SwiftExpenses on a Mac, iPhone or iPad (File > Open Library), or its expenses.db in the web app (also on Windows). It shows a yellow “Review copy — read-only” bar, and nothing in it can be changed.

Submitted reports

  • Submitted reports are locked: you can’t add receipts or car mileage to them, change or delete their expenses, change their MS&IE days, or move expenses into them. They are also left out of the trips offered for unassigned receipts. To change a submitted report, choose Submitted > Mark as Not Submitted (web: Not Submitted), make the change, and submit it again.
  • Submitted reports show a green seal in the sidebar, and their date and submission in Summary and at the top of the report.
  • Reports with the same submission name belong together: in Summary, Submission > Select selects them again, for example to export them once more with Export as Zip.
  • To record a submission you made outside SwiftExpenses, select reports in Summary and choose Submission > Mark Selected as Submitted. Mark as Not Submitted undoes it.

Search all receipts

Type in the search field at the top of the sidebar to find receipts in every report and in Unassigned Receipts. Search looks in the vendor, city, route, report name, expense type, remark, file name, dates (for example 03/09/2026) and amounts. Every word you type must match. Click a result to open it.
