See totals and the summary

Totals update as soon as you save a change.

Totals for one report

The bottom of a report shows the total for each expense type (Air & Transportation, Lodging, Fuel/MLG, Meals & Tips, ENTMT, Other), then the receipts total, the MS&IE total and the combined total. Expense types not in that list, such as Parking, count as Other.

Totals for all reports

Click Summary at the top of the sidebar. It lists each report with its number of receipts, receipts total, MS&IE total and report total, with the grand total at the bottom. The sidebar also shows each report’s total under its name.

Export the summary

In Summary, click the Export Summary to Excel button in the toolbar. The workbook has a summary sheet and one sheet per report with its expenses by type and its MS&IE days.
