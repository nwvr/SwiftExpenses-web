Collect receipts now, assign them to a trip later

Unassigned Receipts is a place for receipts that aren’t on a trip yet. Each receipt is read when you add it, so later you only need to pick the trip.

Upload Receipts

Click Upload Receipts at the bottom of the sidebar (Mac: also File > Upload Receipts, ⌘U; web: at the top of the sidebar). Every receipt you add goes to Unassigned Receipts:

  • Scan with Camera (iPhone and iPad): photograph paper receipts; each scan becomes a PDF.
  • Choose Files: receipt PDFs or photos (a photo becomes a one-page PDF receipt), or a receipts file shared from the iPhone app (see below).
  • Find in Mail (Mac): ride, flight, hotel and car rental receipts from Apple Mail. See Find receipts in Mail.

The guide shows how many receipts were added. You can also add receipts in Unassigned Receipts itself: on the Mac with Choose Files or by dragging files onto the window, on iPhone and iPad with Scan and Choose File at the bottom of the start screen, and on the web with Add Receipts.

Share receipts from the iPhone (for example to Windows)

With iCloud sync, receipts you capture on the iPhone appear on your Mac by themselves. To hand receipts to a computer without iCloud, such as a Windows PC with the web app:

  1. On the iPhone or iPad, open Unassigned Receipts and tap Share Receipts (Mac: File > Share Receipts).
  2. Select the receipts (all are selected) and tap Share. They are sent as one file, such as iPhone captures 2026-10-25.db, with AirDrop, Mail, Messages, OneDrive or Files.
  3. On the other computer, choose Upload Receipts > Choose Files and pick that file. Its receipts are added to Unassigned Receipts with their PDFs and details; receipts already in the library are skipped.

The web app can do the same: tick receipts in Unassigned Receipts and click Save Checked as File (or Save All as File).

Receipts that are already in the library are skipped. The list shows each receipt’s date, vendor and amount, and the trip whose dates it matches; this is updated whenever trips change. A receipt with the same amount as an expense already on a trip, within a day, is marked Possible duplicate, so you can delete it instead of assigning it twice.

Assign a receipt to a trip

  1. Select the receipt.
  2. Choose the trip. The trip whose dates match the receipt comes first, then the trips nearest in time. To start a trip, choose New Trip and type its name.
  3. Check the details, then click Assign to Trip.

Delete receipts

  • Mac: select one or more receipts (hold ⌘ or ⇧ to select several) and press Delete, or use the Delete menu in the toolbar to delete the selected receipts, all receipts from Mail, all uploaded files, or everything.
  • iPhone and iPad: swipe left on a receipt, or use the Delete menu.
  • Web: tick the receipts and click Delete Checked, or click Delete All.
Note: Unassigned receipts aren’t counted in any totals or exports. In the earlier Python app they appear as a report called “Unassigned Receipts”.
