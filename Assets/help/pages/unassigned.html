Collect receipts now, assign them to a trip later

Unassigned Receipts is a place for receipts that aren’t on a trip yet. Each receipt is read when you add it, so later you only need to pick the trip.

Add receipts

  • Mac: select Unassigned Receipts in the sidebar, then click Add Receipt PDFs (you can choose several at once) or drag PDFs onto the window.
  • iPhone and iPad: at the bottom of the start screen, tap Scan to photograph a paper receipt with the camera, or Choose File to pick receipt PDFs or photos in Files. They are read and added to Unassigned Receipts. In New Expense, the same two choices are at the top: Scan with Camera and Choose File.
  • Web: click Unassigned Receipts, then Add Receipt PDFs. Photos of receipts work too (on a phone, the browser also offers the camera).
  • A photo (JPEG, HEIC, PNG) is turned into a one-page PDF receipt.

Receipts that are already in the library are skipped. The list shows each receipt’s date, vendor and amount, and the trip whose dates it matches; this is updated whenever trips change. A receipt with the same amount as an expense already on a trip, within a day, is marked Possible duplicate, so you can delete it instead of assigning it twice.

Assign a receipt to a trip

  1. Select the receipt.
  2. Choose the trip. The trip whose dates match the receipt comes first, then the trips nearest in time. To start a trip, choose New Trip and type its name.
  3. Check the details, then click Assign to Trip.

Delete receipts

  • Mac: select one or more receipts (hold ⌘ or ⇧ to select several) and press Delete, or use the Delete menu in the toolbar to delete the selected receipts, all receipts from Mail, all uploaded files, or everything.
  • iPhone and iPad: swipe left on a receipt, or use the Delete menu.
  • Web: tick the receipts and click Delete Checked, or click Delete All.
Note: Unassigned receipts aren’t counted in any totals or exports. In the earlier Python app they appear as a report called “Unassigned Receipts”.
